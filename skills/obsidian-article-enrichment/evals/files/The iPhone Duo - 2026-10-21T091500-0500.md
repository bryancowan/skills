Apple sent me an iPhone Duo a week ago. It costs $2,000, which is the first thing everyone asks about, and the second thing everyone asks is whether it is a phone or a tablet. After a week, my answer is that it is a brilliant tablet that happens to fit in a pocket, and the worst iPhone I have ever used.

## Closed

With the Duo folded, the outer display is cramped. It is thick and heavy for a phone, and the wide shape makes one-handed use awkward. ==There is no Face ID, and for me that is a dealbreaker.== Unlocking with an Apple Watch helps, but only when the watch is on my wrist and the phone is in a mood to cooperate.

## Open

Unfolded, the 7.6-inch inner display is the best tablet experience Apple has shipped. The nano-texture finish is lovely, and it hides the crease better than I expected. ==At $2,000 it is a luxury, but as a tablet that I can carry everywhere it is the first foldable I have wanted to use.==

## Verdict

Apple has built an extraordinarily polished prototype. I would not spend $2,000 on it, and I would not carry it as my only phone. If the next version brings back Face ID and trims a few millimeters, I would be first in line.
