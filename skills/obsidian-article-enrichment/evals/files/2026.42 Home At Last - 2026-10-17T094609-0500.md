## Apple comes home
After a decade away from home hardware, Apple launched HomeView, a seven-inch always-on display meant to anchor the kitchen counter. ==The open question is how big the market is for an expensive device whose headline feature, Siri, still lags rivals.== Thompson walked back part of his critique of the launch video after explaining the strategy on Sharp Tech.

## SpaceX keeps buying spectrum
SpaceX agreed to buy roughly $8 billion of low-band spectrum. ==What started as emergency satellite texting now looks like a bid to be a primary carrier==, and both carriers and airlines have answered with deals of their own.
