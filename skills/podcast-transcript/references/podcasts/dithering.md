# Podcast Profile: Dithering

Two-person show: Ben Thompson and John Gruber, ~15 minutes per episode
("Not a minute less, not a minute more").

## Speakers

- **Ben Thompson** — Stratechery. Lives in Taiwan (references his own
  Wi-Fi/household setup there as a personal-life anecdote). Business/product
  strategy analyst: long analytical passages about industry trends, "I predict"
  framing, direct-address openers ("[Name],").
- **John Gruber** — Daring Fireball. Frequently the one physically present at
  Apple events ("I only got a few minutes to play with it hands-on", "yesterday",
  "flying home"), gives firsthand hands-on impressions, references his own
  blog/readers by name ("readers of Daring Fireball").

Attribution note: both hosts are experienced analysts, so register/analysis
alone doesn't reliably separate them — the strongest signals are direct address
by name, and self-referencing details (Ben: Taiwan; John: attending the event
in person, Daring Fireball readership).
