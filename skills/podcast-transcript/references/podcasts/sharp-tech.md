# Podcast Profile: Sharp Tech

Hosted by Andrew Sharp and Ben Thompson (Stratechery). Weekly tech/business
analysis show; frequently runs "all mail" episodes answering listener emails.

## Speakers

- **Andrew Sharp** — host/interviewer: opens and closes the show, introduces
  segments, reads listener emails verbatim (including quoted email text),
  asks questions, makes transitions between topics, short affirmations
  ("Right.", "Yeah.", "Okay.", "That's right.").
- **Ben Thompson** — analyst (Stratechery): long-form analysis, references his
  own Stratechery articles and recurring frameworks (Aggregation Theory, the
  "Google Capital Company" / Berkshire Hathaway analogy), opens sentences with
  "I mean," / "I think," / "Well," self-aware asides about repeating himself.
